## About me

I'm a Full Stack Developer working in technology and software development at **PRONIX**, where I helped build the company's technology operation from the ground up.

Together with the team, I've worked on an internal ERP that centralizes the company's operations, an issue tracker used by the development team, and integrations connecting marketplaces, messaging providers, authentication services, and other platforms.

I work across the entire software lifecycle — from understanding the problem and designing the architecture to development, deployment, monitoring, and maintenance.

My main focus is **TypeScript, Node.js, React, PostgreSQL, automation, and AI-powered solutions**, running on self-hosted infrastructure.

I also work with **code audits, security reviews, performance optimization, observability, and AI agents** to automate repetitive processes and improve the systems I build.

> Most of the systems I work on professionally are private repositories.
> What you find here represents the public side of my work: personal projects, experiments, and tools.
